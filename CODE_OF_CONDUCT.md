# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Discuss code, not people.

## Expected

- Keep discussion technical and specific. "This loses money when the book is
  thin" is useful; "this is garbage" is not.
- Assume the other person knows something you do not. In this codebase, most
  odd-looking decisions are odd because reality required it.
- Respect that contributors are not native English speakers. Judge the argument,
  not the grammar.

## Not accepted

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Demanding unpaid support or free work. Questions are welcome; entitlement is
  not.
- Using issues or discussions to promote unrelated products or to recruit users
  for schemes.

## Financial discussion

This project sits next to real money, which attracts a particular kind of noise.
Additionally not accepted:

- soliciting funds, managing other people's money, or offering "guaranteed"
  returns anywhere in this repository;
- posting wallet addresses with claims of performance that cannot be verified
  on-chain;
- asking others to trade on your behalf.

## Enforcement

Report problems to **andreyschurko@gmail.com**. Reports stay confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts. For a
first offence the usual response is a request to stop; for repeated or severe
behaviour, a permanent block without further discussion.
