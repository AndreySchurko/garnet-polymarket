## Who needs one

You need a commercial license if any of the following is true:

- the software is used by, or on behalf of, a **legal entity** — company, fund,
  partnership, trust, DAO with a legal wrapper, or equivalent;
- it trades, manages or advises on **funds belonging to third parties**;
- its output — signals, fills, reports, derived data — is **provided to third
  parties** for a fee or other consideration;
- you are a **proprietary trading firm, market maker, broker or asset manager**,
  of any size.

Earning a profit while trading your own money does **not** make your use
commercial. A one-person operation trading a personal account stays free even
if that account is doing very well.

Not sure which side of the line you are on? Ask. A one-line description of your
setup is enough for a straight answer, and the answer is free.

and evaluate it internally before you talk to anyone about money. Evaluation
